# Online ordering brief

The online menu contains drinks and packaged snacks. Staff sometimes handles off-menu requests after the bar manager confirms availability and price, but whether the online system may take such requests is undecided. The bar manager owns that online-policy decision.

Customers have used these examples: “星云特调” is not yet identifiable as a specific menu item; “蛋炒饭” is a known off-menu request; House lager is listed but currently has zero stock; sparkling water is listed and has five units available.

The existing online flow accepts an order after it reserves stock and returns an order number. Payment comes later. Inventory synchronization can reveal a shortage after acceptance. The policy for cancellation, substitution, or refund in that situation is unresolved; the operations manager owns that decision.
